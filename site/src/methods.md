# Methods (summary)

The full estimand definitions live in `docs/methods.md` in the repository. In short:

- **Stage durations from dockets**: Kaplan–Meier by filing-year cohort; open files censored at the observation date; files closed by a competing event censored at that event; medians, p75, p90 with bootstrap 95% intervals.
- **Age of pending cases from stock–flow tables**: Little's law, W = L / λ, stated as a steady-state approximation of the mean; published only where the stock–flow identity reconciles within 1% of the stock.
- **Reserve time**: last hearing day to decision date from published decisions; medians and p90 with bootstrap intervals; written dispositions excluded.
- **Court-published figures** (StatCan medians, SCC and ONCA averages, OCJ averages) are reported as published and labelled as such.
- **Resource linkage**: irregular vacancy snapshots carried forward monthly, with the snapshot date shown.

Every chart states its definition, n, uncertainty, data vintage, source and bias.
