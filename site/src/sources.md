Durations and counts from the court-file searches are our own derivations, not the courts' statistics. The searches were read slowly (one request every three to four seconds, one at a time), and no party names, entry texts or file numbers are published.

Not used: Superior Court of Justice booking data, daily court lists, CanLII texts, the Ontario Courts Public Portal.

## Limitations to keep in mind

- **Endings missed.** A few percent of files end without the Registry's marker or a discontinuance. We count them as open, so upper percentiles are slightly overstated and our counts of pending files run above the courts' own. Pending totals on this site come from the courts' tables.
- **Published decisions are a subset.** Most Tax Court files, and many Federal Court files, end without published reasons. Measures built on decisions describe a selected group, and coverage of immigration decisions is thinner for files filed in 2012–2015.
- **Entry labels are read by pattern rules.** Hearing and decision entries are classified from the court's wording in English and French; some hearing entries state no subject.

Verified URLs, robots findings and per-source data notes: `docs/sources.md` in the repository.
