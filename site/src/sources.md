# Sources and limitations

| Source | Coverage used | Licence / terms |
|---|---|---|
| Statistics Canada, Integrated Criminal Court Survey (tables 35-10-0027, -0029, -0172 to -0176) | 1994/95–2024/25 | Statistics Canada Open Licence |
| Statistics Canada, Civil Court Survey (35-10-0112, -0114) | counts only | Statistics Canada Open Licence |
| A2AJ Canadian Case Law (SCC, FCA, FC, TCC, ONCA decisions) | derived fields only; texts not republished | A2AJ work MIT; upstream court terms apply |
| Refugee Law Lab, Luck of the Draw III | FC immigration dockets 1997–2022 | CC BY-NC 4.0 |
| Supreme Court of Canada statistics pages | 2002–2025 | Government of Canada non-commercial reproduction |
| Court of Appeal for Ontario Annual Report 2023–2024 | 2014–2024 | Ontario Courts publication |
| Ontario Court of Justice criminal statistics | 2011–2026 (monthly), 2012–2026 (annual) | Ministry of the Attorney General data; the Court disclaims accuracy |
| Federal Court and Federal Court of Appeal activity summaries; Tax Court workload table | FC 2003–2026, FCA 2019–2026, TCC 2018-19–2022-23 | Government of Canada non-commercial reproduction |
| Commissioner for Federal Judicial Affairs, judges and vacancies (via the Internet Archive) | 2009–2026, irregular | Government of Canada non-commercial reproduction |

Not used: Superior Court of Justice booking data, daily court lists, CanLII texts, the Ontario Courts Public Portal.
